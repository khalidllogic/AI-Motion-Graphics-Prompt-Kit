# 🎬 AI Motion Graphics Prompt Kit

**Create engaging motion graphics videos with AI — from a visual reference and a voice-over to a structured video production workflow.**

By [Khalid Logic](https://github.com/khalidllogic)

## 🌟 About the Project

AI Motion Graphics Prompt Kit is a collection of prompts and practical instructions designed to help content creators produce dynamic 2D motion graphics videos using AI tools.

The workflow combines visual reference analysis, voice-over understanding, scene planning, motion design, typography, transitions, and audio-visual synchronization.

You don't need to be a professional motion designer to get started.

## ✨ What Can You Do With It?

- 🎨 Analyze the visual language of a reference video.
- 🎙️ Adapt the visuals to your own voice-over.
- 🎬 Plan scenes, transitions, and motion graphics.
- 📝 Generate detailed prompts for AI-assisted video production.
- ✂️ Work with short segments and assemble them in a video editor.
- 🚀 Develop a repeatable workflow for technical content, educational videos, and social media reels.

## 🧰 Tools

- **ChatGPT:** Analyze the reference video and prepare a detailed production prompt.
- **Gemini:** Use the prompt and your voice-over to develop the video, subject to the available features of your account.
- **CapCut or another video editor:** Assemble clips, synchronize audio, and polish the final result.

Tool capabilities and usage limits may vary by account and subscription.

## 📂 Repository Structure

- `prompts/master-prompt.md` — The complete motion graphics production prompt.
- `prompts/quick-prompt.md` — A shorter prompt for quick experiments.
- `examples/usage-guide.md` — A practical step-by-step workflow.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Choose a reference video that you have permission to use.
2. Upload it to ChatGPT and analyze its general visual characteristics.
3. Copy the generated production prompt and customize it for your own content.
4. Provide the prompt, reference material, and voice-over to a suitable video generation tool.
5. If necessary, produce shorter segments and assemble them in a video editor.
6. Review the final result and refine the timing, typography, transitions, and audio synchronization.

## 💡 Pro Tip

The goal isn't to copy another creator's video frame by frame.

Instead, identify the general visual principles that make it effective — pacing, composition, color, typography, and motion — and adapt those principles to your own original content.

Always respect copyright and the rights associated with reference materials.
